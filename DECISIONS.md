# Decisions

Design verdicts Joe gave, newest first. Read before changing the look.

## 2026-11-19: marker rebrand, B + D

Five options built on Lera Lesyk's Stop-Motion Highlighter (brik.space): A highlighter
notebook, B blackout in the current brand, C highlight-as-earned, D identity as a
blackout poem, E correction tape at night. Joe picked **B and D**.

- B: keep Archivo, hard rules, black and white. Words that wait sit under grainy marker
  (black in light mode, white in dark) that boils at about 8 fps and wipes off in steps.
- D: the marker is the identity. The home lede is redacted down to "Stay a while," and
  uncovers as you wait; each poem row previews its first line under marker and lifts on
  hover; social card and favicon are marker bars.
- Rejected: A and E (they drop the brand), C (highlighting instead of covering).
